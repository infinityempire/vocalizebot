Questions you probably have

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. The text-back runs on a forwarding number that points to your existing line, or we use your current business number — your choice. We go over this on the setup call.

Will it look like spam to my customers?

No. The reply is short, signed by you, and only fires when a real call is missed — not on every contact. It reads like a human, because it's written like one.

What if I answer the phone?

Then nothing happens. The bot only kicks in when a call goes unanswered. If you pick up, it stays out of the way.

How fast can I be live?

If there's a pilot spot open, usually the same day you say yes. Setup takes about 10–15 minutes on a quick call.

Is this a real person or a bot?

Both. The bot handles the instant text-back so no lead goes cold. I (Tal) personally handle setup, questions, and anything that needs a human. You can always reach me directly.
